About TB

Tuberculosis (TB) is an infectious disease that mainly affects the lungs. It spreads through the air when an infected person coughs or sneezes.

Early screening and timely medical attention can significantly reduce complications and transmission.

General Precautions

  • Cover mouth while coughing
  • Maintain proper ventilation
  • Avoid smoking and alcohol
  • Eat nutritious food regularly
  • Consult a doctor if cough persists for more than 2 weeks

Why Early Screening Matters

Early screening helps identify possible TB symptoms sooner, improving chances of timely medical intervention and recovery.

How AI Helps

Artificial Intelligence analyzes cough sound patterns and chest X-rays to identify possible TB-related abnormalities.

Audio processing techniques convert cough sounds into spectrograms which are then analyzed using trained models.

Combining cough analysis with radiology data enables a more reliable multimodal screening approach.
