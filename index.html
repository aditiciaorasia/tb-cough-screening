About TB

Tuberculosis (TB) is an infectious disease that mainly affects the lungs. It spreads through the air when an infected person coughs or sneezes.

Early screening and timely medical attention can significantly reduce complications and transmission.

General Precautions

  • Cover mouth while coughing
  • Maintain proper ventilation
  • Avoid smoking and alcohol
  • Eat nutritious food regularly
  • Consult a doctor if cough persists for more than 2 weeks
